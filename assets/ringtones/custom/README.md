# Os meus toques

Paste `.wav` files here to use them as doorbell ringtones. They appear in
Definições › Campainha under "Os meus toques", named after the file
(`Toque da avó.wav` → "Toque da avó"), and play in a loop while the
doorbell rings, until it's answered or the call ends (30 s at most).

- Any PCM `.wav` works (any sample rate, mono or stereo); they're
  converted when played.
- Keep them short and small: they're bundled into the app. A few seconds
  that loop cleanly sound best.
- Loudness is the file's own; the volume slider scales it.
- They reach the screen with the next build and deploy to the Pi.
